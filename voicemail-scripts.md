# Ringless Voicemail Scripts

These scripts are written for AI automation agency cold outreach.
Keep recordings to **20–30 seconds** — anything longer gets ignored.

**Recording tips:**
- Record in a quiet room, use your phone's voice memo app
- Speak naturally, not like you're reading
- Smile while recording — it comes through in your voice
- Save as MP3 and upload to a public URL before running the workflow

---

## Script 1: The Simple Callback (Best for First-Time Use)

**Best for:** Any niche. Universal. Highest callback rate.

> "Hey, this is [Your Name] — I just left you a quick message. I help local [niche]
> businesses in [city] save time and get more customers using AI automation —
> things like automatic follow-ups, review requests, and missed-call text-back.
> Takes about 15 minutes to show you how it works. Give me a call back at
> [your number] when you get a chance. Again, that's [your number]. Thanks!"

**Length:** ~22 seconds

---

## Script 2: The Problem/Solution Hook

**Best for:** Service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, contractors)

> "Hey [Business Name], this is [Your Name]. Quick question — are you still
> following up with every lead that calls your business? Most contractors I talk to
> are losing 30 to 40 percent of jobs just because no one followed up in time.
> I built a simple AI system that does it automatically. Takes 15 minutes to set up.
> Happy to show you for free. Call me back at [your number]. That's [your number].
> Talk soon."

**Length:** ~28 seconds

---

## Script 3: The Social Proof Angle

**Best for:** Any niche, once you have a client result

> "Hey, this is [Your Name] — I recently helped a [niche] in [city] add an extra
> [X leads / $X revenue / Y hours saved] per month using a simple AI system that
> handles their follow-ups and appointment reminders automatically. I wanted to
> see if it would work for your business too. No cost to find out — just a
> 15-minute call. Reach me at [your number]. That's [your number]. Thanks!"

**Length:** ~25 seconds

---

## Script 4: The Missed Call Problem (High-Intent)

**Best for:** Any service business that relies on inbound calls

> "Hey, this is [Your Name]. Did you know the average small business misses
> about 30 percent of inbound calls — and most of those people never call back?
> I help local businesses automatically text back every missed call so you never
> lose a lead again. It's running in the background 24/7 — you don't touch it.
> I'd love to show you how it works. Call me back at [your number]. Again,
> [your number]. Have a great day."

**Length:** ~28 seconds

---

## Script 5: Short & Punchy (For High Volume Campaigns)

**Best for:** When you're blasting 100+ VMs and want maximum callbacks

> "Hey, this is [Your Name]. I help [niche] businesses in [city] get more
> customers on autopilot using AI. If that's interesting, give me a call back
> at [your number]. That's [your number]. Thanks."

**Length:** ~14 seconds

## Follow-Up Script (Send 3–5 Days After First VM)

Only send this to leads who haven't called back.

> "Hey, [Business Name] — this is [Your Name] again. I left you a voicemail
> last week about automating your follow-ups and getting more customers without
> extra work. I know you're busy — if there's ever a good time for a 15-minute
> call, I'd love to connect. You can reach me at [your number]. No pressure at
> all. Hope you're having a great week."

**Length:** ~25 seconds

## Key Rules for a Good VM

1. **Say your number twice** — people listen once, need to hear it again to write it down
2. **Keep it under 30 seconds** — anything longer and they stop listening
3. **One clear ask** — "call me back" not "call me, text me, email me, visit my website"
4. **Never say "AI automation agency"** in the VM — say what the outcome is instead
5. **Personalize the niche** — "I help plumbers" beats "I help businesses"
6. **Sound casual, not scripted** — record 5 takes and pick the most natural one
7. **Don't mention price** — the goal of the VM is to get a callback, not close a sale

## Hosting Your Audio File

After recording, host your MP3 at one of these:

| Option | How | Direct URL? |
|---|---|---|
| Google Drive | Upload → Share → "Anyone with link" → copy link, change `/view` to `/uc?export=download` | Yes |
| Dropbox | Upload → Share → change `?dl=0` to `?dl=1` at end of URL | Yes |
| Amazon S3 | Upload to public bucket → copy object URL | Yes |
| Your web host | Upload to `/public` folder → direct URL | Yes |

Test your URL by pasting it in an incognito browser tab — it should download/play the MP3.
